Our Story

BUILT ON VOLTAGE & VISION

AG Electric Works was founded on a singular mission: to deliver world-class electrical solutions with integrity, precision, and professionalism. From humble beginnings in Karachi, we've grown into one of the region's most respected electrical contracting firms.

Under the leadership of Mr. Abdul Ghaffar (CEO), our team has powered residential towers, commercial complexes, industrial plants, and critical infrastructure — bringing light, safety, and efficiency wherever we go.

Every cable we lay and every panel we commission carries the full weight of our reputation. We don't just meet code — we exceed expectations.

What We Do

FULL SPECTRUM ELECTRICAL SOLUTIONS

🏭

Industrial Installations

High-voltage switchgear, motor control centers, transformer installations, and complete electrical systems for factories and industrial complexes.

🏢

Commercial Projects

Office buildings, shopping centers, hotels, and corporate campuses — full electrical design, supply, and installation with zero compromise.

🔌

Power Distribution

LT/HT panel boards, cable tray systems, earthing & bonding, and complete power distribution networks engineered for reliability.

🔦

Lighting Systems

Energy-efficient LED solutions, emergency lighting, smart controls, and architectural lighting for all project scales.

⚙️

Maintenance & Testing

Preventive maintenance contracts, electrical testing, thermal imaging, and 24/7 emergency response to keep your systems online.

📋

Consultancy & Design

Electrical consultancy, load analysis, single-line diagrams, and compliance audits — from concept through completion.
